About FontDale

Our Story

FontDale was first conceptualized in 2023 by founder Lord Alvaro, a visionary entrepreneur with a passion for architecture, culture, and elevated retail experiences. Frustrated by the sameness of conventional shopping centers, Alvaro set out to create a destination that combined global retail with genuine artistry.

After two years of meticulous planning, design, and construction, FontDale officially opened its doors in 2025. Since then, it has quickly earned its reputation as the premier premium lifestyle mall in the region — a place where every detail, from the marble flooring to the curated tenant mix, reflects a commitment to quality and elegance.

Our Founder

Lord Alvaro is an entrepreneur and design enthusiast who built his career at the intersection of architecture and retail development. With a background in urban planning and a deep appreciation for contemporary design, Lord Alvaro envisioned FontDale as more than a mall — a living space where community, culture, and commerce converge.

"FontDale is not just a place to shop. It is a place to experience life at its finest," says Alvaro. His guiding philosophy — that premium environments inspire premium moments — is reflected in every corner of the property.

What Makes Us Unique

FontDale is built around three pillars that set it apart from any other mall in the region:

Curated Tenancy. Every store, restaurant, and service provider in FontDale is hand-selected to ensure alignment with our vision of quality and modernity. We do not simply fill space — we build an ecosystem.

Architectural Excellence. Designed by internationally recognized architects, every inch of FontDale is purposeful. Vaulted ceilings, natural lighting, and premium materials create an environment that is as beautiful as it is functional.

The Lumen Skywalk. The signature connecting bridge between the North Gallery and South Pavilion is an experience in itself — a glass-encased walkway with rotating art installations and panoramic views of Arcadia Circle.

Our Wings

North Gallery

Home to fashion, beauty, and lifestyle brands. The North Gallery is a curated retail journey through some of the world's most recognized names in style and design.

South Pavilion

A hub for technology, luxury, and dining. The South Pavilion houses premium brands and a vibrant food hall that brings international culinary experiences under one roof.
