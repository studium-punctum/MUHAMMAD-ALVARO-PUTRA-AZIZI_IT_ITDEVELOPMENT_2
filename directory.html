Experience the full range of Apple products and accessories. The FontDale Apple Store features dedicated Genius Bar support, hands-on product demos, and expert staff to assist with all your tech needs.

BOOKS & LIFESTYLE · NORTH GALLERY

Asia's premier bookstore, Kinokuniya at FontDale offers an extensive collection of international books, magazines, stationery, and gifts. A quiet retreat for the intellectually curious within the mall.
